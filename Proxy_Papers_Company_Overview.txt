Who we are


Proxy Papers is a French premium service founded in 2016, dedicated to the private management of personal data and documents for high-net-worth individuals. We operate at the intersection of human expertise and sovereign technology — a space that, until now, had no dedicated player.
Our clients have built structured wealth. They are well-advised — tax attorneys, notaries, CPAs, wealth managers, private bankers. Yet one critical layer is consistently missing: a trusted professional whose sole responsibility is to organise, secure, and maintain the full documentary and data ecosystem of their private life. That is what Proxy Papers provides.
Your wealth is structured. Your data rarely is. That gap is where everything becomes complicated.
Our founding story
The company was founded after a direct personal experience: the founder's grandfather suffered from Alzheimer's disease, and when he passed, the absence of organised personal documentation turned an already difficult succession into an extended ordeal. Proxy Papers was born from the conviction that this kind of chaos — preventable, costly, emotionally taxing — should never be inevitable.
The founder's background in the accounting sector and a subsequent venture in GDPR compliance consulting (Optimex Data, 2017–2023) gave the company a rare combination of expertise: intimacy with the advisory ecosystem, a deep understanding of data sovereignty issues, and a practical grasp of what high-net-worth individuals actually need — and currently lack.
Where we stand today
In 2026, Proxy Papers operates from its headquarters in Grenoble and a second branch in Toulon. The company is relaunching with a reinforced positioning, a dedicated technology platform (Papers Box), and a clear focus on the premium segment. A network of brand licensees operated between 2016 and 2021; the current strategy is direct delivery with the highest service standards.

The problem we solve


High-net-worth individuals face a structural blind spot. Their financial, legal, and tax affairs are handled by qualified professionals. But the documentary layer that underpins all of it — the contracts, notarial deeds, insurance policies, SCI leases, beneficiary clauses, tax filings, succession documents — lives nowhere coherently. It is scattered across emails, filing cabinets, cloud drives, and the memories of trusted assistants who may leave.
This creates recurring, measurable costs that are rarely quantified but universally experienced.

Our answer
Proxy Papers fills this gap with a structured, ongoing service that combines a dedicated human advisor with a certified sovereign technology platform. We are not a storage tool. We are not a financial advisor. We are the connective tissue between a client's private life and the professionals who serve it.
OUR PROMISE
A dedicated advisor. Sovereign technology. — Un conseiller dédié. Une technologie souveraine.
	Concretely: a Proxy Papers advisor comes to the client, collects and organises all private documents and data, structures them in the Papers Box (our proprietary platform, hosted in France), sets up secure sharing with the client's advisors, and provides ongoing maintenance — month after month, year after year. Every deadline is tracked. Every document is current. Every advisor gets what they need, when they need it.
What we do not do
We do not provide tax, legal, or financial advice. We do not manage assets. We do not replace the client's notary, CPA, attorney, or wealth manager. We make those professionals more effective by ensuring their client arrives prepared.

Phase 2 — Annual subscription

The referral partner model
Proxy Papers operates two distinct partnership models, reflecting the professional realities of our two referral segments.
Regulated professions — referral between peers
For notaries, CPAs, and attorneys, we operate a reciprocal referral model with no financial commission. These professions are subject to deontological rules that prohibit or severely restrict commission-based referrals. Our relationship with them is based on mutual benefit: a better-organised client saves the professional significant time, improves the quality of their advisory work, and strengthens the client relationship. We list their firm; they recommend Proxy Papers where appropriate.
Advisors and introducers — business referral
For wealth managers (CGP), brokers, insurers, and family offices, we offer a formal business introduction agreement. A referral fee is paid on the first year of subscription — the precise rate is agreed contractually on a case-by-case basis. The arrangement is fully transparent to the end client and governed by a written convention.
White-label and licensing
Proxy Papers is open to exploring white-label arrangements for partners who wish to integrate the Papers Box platform and associated advisory service into their own client offering, under their brand. This model is subject to minimum volume commitments, a formal agreement, and quality standards alignment. Interested parties should contact the founding team directly.
